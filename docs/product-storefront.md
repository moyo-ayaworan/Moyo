# Managing digital products

Open **Admin → Digital Products**. Every product has its own page; MELATONE is one product in the catalogue, not a special case in the code.

1. Choose **Photography** or **Fine Art**. This determines which shop contains the product.
2. Set its title, NGN price, short details and optional cover image.
3. Upload the paid file under **Protected Delivery File**. This file is private and is released through the existing verified-payment download flow.
4. Open **Product page content** to add a product type, introduction, full description, contents, compatibility, file formats, installation steps and licence terms. List fields use one item per line.
5. Add optional gallery images and before-and-after examples. These images are public previews: never upload the paid original here. Comparisons should use matching framing and dimensions.
6. Existing products have **Replace thumbnail**, **Remove thumbnail**, **Replace delivery file** and **Remove delivery file** controls. Removing a thumbnail uses the standard placeholder. Asset changes are saved with the product; uploaded images do not require you to paste a URL.
7. Save with **Add product** or **Update product**. Use **View saved product page** to review the published result. Empty optional sections stay hidden.

Each shop automatically shows its active products. With several products, visitors can search and filter by the product types you enter. Related products stay within the same collection. Unpublished products cannot be opened through their product page.

Changing the title updates the readable part of its URL; older title URLs redirect using the product ID. Changing the collection moves the product to that shop, and the old collection URL becomes unavailable.

MELATONE’s price remains ₦20,000. No product specifications, licence claims or sample results are supplied automatically; add your own accurate content in admin.
