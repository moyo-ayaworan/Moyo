STILLBOUNDS — FIRST-TIME GUIDE
by Moyo Ayaworan

START HERE — keep this guide open while you install and try your first book.
This guide is included in the ZIP beside Stillbounds.app. In TextEdit, use Command+F to
find a topic. A label such as File > Save means click File in the app menu, then Save.
Cmd and the symbol ⌘ both mean the Command key; Shift is ⇧; Esc is Escape. A spread is
two facing pages.

INSTALL OR UPDATE ON YOUR MAC
1. Check your Mac: click the Apple symbol at the top-left of the screen, then About This
Mac. Choose the Apple Silicon download for an Apple M-series chip, or Intel for an Intel
processor. Apple Silicon needs macOS 26.2 or later; Intel needs macOS 13.3 or later.
2. Find the download: click Finder (the blue smiling-face icon in the Dock), then
Downloads in the left sidebar. Wait until the ZIP has finished downloading before
opening it. You only need the ZIP for your Mac edition.
3. Unzip: double-click the Stillbounds ZIP. Finder extracts a folder containing
Stillbounds.app and Stillbounds-First-Time-Guide.txt. Double-click the text file to read
it in TextEdit. No separate guide download is needed.
4. If updating: open your existing Stillbounds, save your book with Command+S, then
choose Stillbounds > Quit Stillbounds in the top menu bar, or press Command+Q. Close the
app before replacing it.
5. Install: in Finder, drag Stillbounds.app from the extracted folder onto Applications
in the left sidebar. Drag means hold the mouse or trackpad button on the app, move it
onto Applications, then release. If Finder asks about an existing copy, choose Replace.
Do not move your photo folders or .mofolio projects.
6. Launch the installed copy: click Applications in Finder and double-click Stillbounds.
If you cannot find Applications in the sidebar, choose Go > Applications from Finder's
menu bar at the top of the screen. Launch from Applications rather than from the
download folder.
7. If Apple cannot check the app or the developer cannot be verified: for a copy
downloaded from the official Stillbounds website, choose Done (or Cancel) on that
warning, rather than Move to Bin / Move to Trash.
8. Then click the Apple symbol > System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll down to
Security, find the notice about Stillbounds, and click Open Anyway. Enter your Mac login
password or use Touch ID if requested, then click Open in the confirmation. This is your
Mac authentication, not your Stillbounds account password. macOS normally remembers this
exception for later launches.
9. The Open Anyway steps apply to an unverified-developer or unnotarized-app warning. If
the message says the app is damaged or will damage your computer, or Open Anyway is
unavailable, keep the exact message and contact the developer. Do not delete your
photographs or projects. Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445
10. Activate: follow Stillbounds' sign-in instructions to open the website in your
browser. Register, verify your email, and approve the sign-in code shown by the app.
Return to the Stillbounds window afterwards. First activation and periodic licence
renewal need internet; local editing, saving, analysis and export work offline within
the signed licence period.
11. Keep it easy to find: while Stillbounds is open, Control-click its icon in the Dock,
then choose Options > Keep in Dock. You can also reopen it from Finder > Applications.
12. After installation: keep your guide somewhere easy to find. Once the copy in
Applications opens successfully, the extracted download copy of Stillbounds.app and its
ZIP are optional backups. Removing those download copies does not uninstall the separate
copy in Applications. Never remove your original photo folders, saved .mofolio files, or
matching .assets folders as part of this cleanup.

Your first book: Add photos > Organise photos > Design pages > Review & export

FIND YOUR WAY AROUND
Home holds your books. Add photos imports your images; Organise photos lets you check
folders, order, people and story suggestions. Design pages opens the spread editor.
Review & export helps you check the book and produce local files.
In the editor, the photo library is on the left, your spread is in the centre, and
editing controls appear when you select a photo or text card. The bottom Story strip
shows photographs; Spreads shows page designs. Both scroll sideways. Click a spread
thumbnail to edit it.
Use Preview to read the book. Its Overview, zoom, full-screen and arrow controls help
you navigate. Choose Edit this spread to return to editing. Hover over tools for
shortcut hints; File and Edit menus also show available shortcuts.

YOUR FIRST PRACTICE BOOK — FOLLOW THESE STEPS
1. Put a small set of edited JPEG, PNG or TIFF photographs in a folder you will keep in
place. Stillbounds links to the original files; it does not replace your photo editor or
develop RAW files.
2. On Home, click Start with your photographs. Give the book a name, choose its purpose
and size, then Continue. Add photo folders, or choose photographs individually. Click
Continue after import.
3. Choose Keep my folders to keep your folder order, or Organise for me for Smart mode.
Click Create my book. Let the draft open; background analysis can continue while you
edit. Review suggestions rather than treating them as confirmed facts.
4. In Design pages, click a spread thumbnail in the bottom strip. Click a photograph on
the large spread to reveal Crop, Colour, Frame, Swap and More. Use Layout & spacing or
Shuffle layout to try another composition. Command+Z undoes a change.
5. Save early: press Command+S. In the Mac save window, choose a folder you can find
again, such as Documents, type a project name and click Save. The .mofolio file stores
your design; linked originals must remain available. Use Command+Shift+S to save a
separate project file.
6. Check Review & export for issues to inspect. Open Preview, use the arrow controls to
turn pages, and choose Edit this spread to return to editing. Escape closes Preview.
7. Click Export book, choose JPEG, PNG or PDF and the required options, then Export
locally. Select an output folder in the Mac chooser. Start with a lightweight proof.
Exported pages or PDFs are delivery files; keep the .mofolio project and originals for
future editing.
8. To continue later, open Stillbounds and select your book on Home, or use Open project
to select the saved .mofolio file. If photos are missing, use the relink instructions
below rather than starting over.

1. GET YOUR PHOTOS READY
Start with a small set of edited photographs so you can learn the workflow comfortably.
- Register and verify your email on the Stillbounds website, then approve your Mac’s
  sign-in code. Your 30-day trial starts on first activation; there is no automatic
  charge. Your name appears in the app after sign-in.
- Use JPEG, PNG or TIFF files. RAW development happens in your photo editor before
  import. Quick import prepares previews first; optional image-label analysis can run
  separately.
- Keep the photographs in a folder you can leave in place. Stillbounds preserves the
  original files and links to their locations.
- Your book can be a wedding or event album, a portrait book, or an art portfolio. You
  can change its setup later.
Remember: A saved book is not a backup of your photographs. Keep your own backup of the
original photo folder.

2. CREATE YOUR FIRST BOOK
On Home, choose Start with your photographs. The creation flow takes you through
purpose, photos and design.
- Choose a book name, type and size first. Add several selected photo folders together
  and arrange their order. Choose Keep my folders (Folder mode) or Organise for me
  (Smart mode), then Create my book. Page settings chooses the length automatically;
  expand it only to set a total or spreads per group. A spread is two facing pages.
- Every creation mode opens an editable draft first, then automatically analyses faces,
  expressions and person groups for all imported photos. Gemma photo categories and
  descriptions are optional: choose Essentials, Photo categories, or Categories &
  descriptions. Keep editing and saving while it runs. Suggestions are used
  automatically. Open Photo assistant, then AI results to change a category or explore
  people. Your corrections take priority; analysis never rearranges your pages. Photo
  analysis stays local; no photo upload is required. Register and verify your email to
  activate the app first.
- Folder mode keeps each folder together and follows your folder order. Smart mode uses
  available story signals to organise your selected photos. Faces, expressions and
  people grouping run in either mode; optional Gemma categories and descriptions stay
  off until enabled.
Remember: Performance offers Auto, Quiet, Balanced and Performance modes. Quiet leaves
more room for other apps. Pause analysis to keep editing; Resume reuses completed
checkpoints. Intel photo AI checks compatible GPUs and falls back to CPU. Follow the
progress message; Stop safely cancels processing, and completed analysis can be reused
when you retry.

3. CHECK THE AI SUGGESTIONS
Treat the first draft as a starting point. Your knowledge of the photographs matters
more than an automatic guess.
- Open Story, then Story settings. Review the suggested category and section; use the
  acceptance buttons or choose your own labels.
- In People & expressions, inspect suggested groups before naming or confirming them.
  You can split, merge or correct groups.
- To analyse an existing book, open Photo assistant, then AI options, or Photo
  suggestions in Organise photos settings. Choose whether to add Gemma, and photos
  already on pages, selected book photos, or the entire library. Faces, expressions and
  grouping remain enabled for the full library. Use Measure this Mac for a small local
  benchmark; estimates use this device and update during processing. No estimate is
  invented for an unmeasured Mac. Important photographs should stay in your selection
  even when a suggestion is wrong. After activation, editing, import, AI, saving and
  export work offline within your signed licence period. Registration, first activation
  and periodic licence renewal need internet.
Remember: Model descriptions and person matches can be wrong. Expression labels describe
visible appearance; they do not establish how someone feels.

4. REFINE THE PAGES
Choose Design to work on the spread. Select a photograph or card to reveal its editing
controls.
- Use Layout to try a different composition. Move cards, adjust the crop, or choose fit
  to show the whole photograph and fill to cover its frame.
- Shift-click selects more than one card. Use Undo (Cmd+Z) and Redo (Cmd+Shift+Z) when
  trying changes.
- Open Cover, then Browse cover designs for eight editable front/back templates using
  photos already in the book. Enter final spine, hinge, board-overhang and wrap
  measurements from your print lab before applying a cover design.
- Lock finished spreads or placements before automatic rearranging. Review crops, text,
  safe margins and faces near the fold.
Remember: Changing the interface between dark and light does not change your book's page
colours or exports.

5. SAVE AND PROTECT YOUR WORK
Press Cmd+S or choose File > Save project. Keep a regular project save even though local
recovery is automatic.
- A linked .mofolio project saves edits and references your original photo files. Keep
  those files in their original folders.
- To move a book to another computer, open Book setup and choose Save portable copy with
  photos. Keep the .mofolio file and its matching .assets folder together.
- Use Version history for available earlier local edits. After an interruption, choose
  Recover last session on Home and select the book and recovery version you need.
Remember: Recovery keeps metadata on this Mac. Force quit or power loss can bypass the
final save, and recovery does not replace an original-photo backup.

6. REVIEW, PREVIEW AND EXPORT
Choose Review to inspect warnings, then Preview to turn through the whole book before
exporting.
- Check page order, every crop, captions, missing sources and low-resolution warnings.
  Return to the relevant spread to make corrections.
- Choose Export book and select JPEG, PNG or PDF. Start with a lightweight proof before
  making a full-resolution export.
- To collect client feedback online, choose Share for review while signed in to your
  Stillbounds account. Publish compressed review pages, then share the private client
  link. On the website, open your profile and My review books to copy links, read and
  resolve feedback, or delete a review. Reviews belong to the account used to publish
  them; original photos and editable projects stay on your Mac. Publishing needs
  internet.
- For printing, confirm the printer's page size, bleed, resolution and colour
  requirements. A screen preview or a clean warning list does not certify print
  compliance.
Remember: You can reopen this guide from Help > First-time guide in the editor, or
First-time guide on Home. Save a text copy to keep beside your project.

QUICK SHORTCUTS
- Cmd+S: save the project.
- Cmd+Shift+S: save the project as a new file.
- Cmd+Z: undo the last edit.
- Cmd+Shift+Z: redo an edit.
- Cmd+I: import individual photographs into the current book.
- Cmd+D: duplicate selected cards on the spread.
- Delete or Backspace: remove selected cards from the spread; original photo files are
  preserved.
- Shift-click: add cards to a selection.
- Arrow keys while a card frame has focus: nudge it; hold Shift for a larger move. In
  crop editing, arrow keys adjust the focused photo's crop instead. Locked cards or
  crops cannot be nudged.
- Left/Right arrows outside editing fields: move between spreads. In Preview, turn to
  the previous/next page.
- Escape: close Preview or the current dialog, or clear the editor selection.
- Tab / Shift+Tab: move keyboard focus forward / backward through controls. Enter or
  Space activates a focused button.

WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG
A photograph is missing
Open Book setup and use Use original photo folder, or select the photograph and choose
Relink / replace source. If the original changed, inspect its dimensions and crop again
before exporting.

Analysis stops or a suggestion is wrong
Retry Analyse photos locally, or organise the photographs manually. Completed
suggestions are cached. Incorrect guesses do not need to be accepted to keep editing
your book.

Saving reports an error
Keep the app open, check free space and access to the save location, and retry.
Background recovery retries automatically. Closing after a failed save offers Keep
editing or an explicit Close without saving.

CHOOSING THE RIGHT MAC VERSION
Use Apple Silicon for M-series Macs and Intel for Intel Macs. Both include offline photo
suggestions, face detection, person-group suggestions and expression analysis. The Intel
AI edition requires macOS 13.3 or later in the Ventura edition and targets 16 GB RAM or
more. Its physical Intel speed and compatibility still need hardware verification.

Source photographs stay local. Online client sharing is a separate, deliberate action
that requires a configured connection; it is not needed for the offline workflow above.
